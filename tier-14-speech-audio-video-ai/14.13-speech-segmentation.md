### 1. WHAT IS IT?

Speech segmentation splits a long audio stream into chunks: speech vs non-speech (voice activity detection, VAD), utterance/endpoint boundaries, or sentence/phrase units, so downstream models see manageable, clean segments.

### 2. WHY DOES IT EXIST?

ASR models have window limits, silence invites hallucination, and transcribing silence wastes money. Segmentation also provides the units for diarization ([14.14](14.14-speaker-diarization.md)) and low-latency endpointing ([14.19](14.19-realtime-speech-systems.md)).

### 3. DETAILED EXPLANATION

**Energy VAD.** Compute per-frame energy; mark frames within `threshold_db` of the loudest frame as speech; add a *hangover* (a few extra frames) so word endings are not clipped; merge flags into segments (`energy_vad`, `flags_to_segments`). Simple, fast, and fooled by non-speech noise.

**Model-based VAD.** Small neural classifiers on log-mel or raw audio (WebRTC GMM VAD, Silero-style models, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`) distinguish speech from music/noise much better.

**Endpointing.** In live systems the question is 'has the user finished?': wait for a silence of N ms after speech. Short N feels responsive but cuts users off; long N adds latency (the 400 ms in Figure 9's budget). Semantic endpointing uses the transcript (is the sentence complete?) (`REVIEW_REQUIRED`).

**Chunking for long audio.** Cut at silences near the target chunk length, add small overlaps, and carry context.

**Parameters.** Threshold, hangover, minimum speech and silence durations. Tune on representative noisy data, not clean studio audio.

### 5. SIMPLE EXAMPLE

A secretary who only starts writing when someone speaks and keeps writing a moment after the last word, so sentence endings are not lost.

### 6. TECHNICAL EXAMPLE

A batch pipeline runs VAD, drops segments under 200 ms, merges segments separated by less than 300 ms, caps chunks at 30 s and sends them to ASR in a batch. The lab's `energy_vad` recovers the single 0.5-1.0 s burst in a test.

### 7. CODE

```python
# run inside lab/
import numpy as np
from audio_core import noise, tone, energy_vad, flags_to_segments

sr = 8000
x = np.concatenate([noise(0.5, sr, 0.001), tone(500, 0.5, sr), noise(0.5, sr, 0.001)])
flags = energy_vad(x, sr, frame_ms=20, threshold_db=-30, hangover=2)
print(flags_to_segments(flags, frame_s=0.02))     # about [(0.5, 1.0..1.1)]
```

### 8. INPUT → PROCESS → OUTPUT

```
long audio ─► VAD ─► speech segments ─► (merge/split to <= window) ─► ASR / diarization
```

### 9. ENTERPRISE USE CASE

Call recordings are mostly silence and hold music; VAD before ASR typically cuts compute materially and removes a major hallucination source (the size of savings depends on the data, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`).

### 10. AI ENGINEERING CONNECTION

VAD gates every streaming speech system and is the first defence against Whisper-style hallucination; it also defines barge-in detection in realtime agents.

### 11. ADVANTAGES

Cheap, reduces cost, improves accuracy, enables streaming endpointing.

### 12. LIMITATIONS

Energy VAD fails on noise and music; hangover/threshold trade clipping vs false alarms; overlapping or whispered speech is hard.

### 13. TRADE-OFFS

Sensitivity vs false alarms; endpoint patience vs responsiveness.

### 14. COMMON MISTAKES

- Tuning on clean audio only.
- Zero hangover (clipped words).
- Hard-cutting mid-word at chunk limits.
- Treating VAD silence as 'no content' for music or tones.

### 15. PERFORMANCE / COST CONSIDERATIONS

Energy VAD is negligible CPU; neural VADs cost milliseconds per frame batch. Saved ASR time often exceeds VAD cost many times.

### 16. SECURITY CONSIDERATIONS

VAD thresholds can be gamed (quiet speech slipping under the threshold; noise bursts triggering recording): important for always-listening devices and consent (record only after wake word, `REVIEW_REQUIRED` for legal specifics).

### 17. INTERVIEW EXPLANATION

*"Speech segmentation finds where speech happens. A VAD flags speech frames, usually by energy or a small neural model, with a hangover so word ends survive; then I merge or split to fit the ASR window. In live systems the endpoint silence threshold trades responsiveness against cutting people off."*

### 18. RAPID-FIRE ANSWER

Detect speech regions and chunk long audio into clean segments, adding hangover to avoid clipping.
