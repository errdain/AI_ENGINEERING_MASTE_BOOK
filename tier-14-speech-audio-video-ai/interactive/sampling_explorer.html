Waveform, sampling-rate and quantization explorer

A continuous sine (grey) is sampled at the chosen rate (dots) and quantized to the chosen bit depth (steps). Push the frequency above half the sample rate (the Nyquist limit) and the reconstructed wave (red) is a lower alias frequency.

Rule: sampling needs sr > 2 x highest frequency (Nyquist). Each extra bit adds about 6 dB of SNR (SNR = 6.02 b + 1.76 dB for a full-scale sine). Speech ASR typically uses 16 kHz because most speech information lies below 8 kHz (REVIEW_REQUIRED: common practice, not a law).
