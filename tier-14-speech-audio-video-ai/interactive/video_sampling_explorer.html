Prices and tokens-per-frame are free parameters of this calculator, not provider quotes (REVIEW_REQUIRED for any real model). Uniform-K guarantees coverage but not temporal density; fixed stride grows cost linearly with length; fast events shorter than the sampling gap can be missed entirely.
