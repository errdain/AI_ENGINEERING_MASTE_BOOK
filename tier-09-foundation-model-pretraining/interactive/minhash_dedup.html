MinHash: for each of the hash functions keep the minimum hash over the shingle set; the chance two sets agree on one slot equals their Jaccard similarity. LSH: two docs become a candidate pair if they agree on all r rows of at least one band, with probability 1-(1-s^r)^b. Hash used here is a simple 32-bit mixer, good enough for a demo (the lab uses blake2b).
