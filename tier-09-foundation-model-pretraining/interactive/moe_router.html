MoE router & load balancing

Watch a top-k router spread tokens over experts; turn the balancing loss on and off, and see capacity overflow (dropped tokens).

Toy router: 4 experts, 2-D tokens in 4 clusters, router logits z = W·x + b trained only by the Switch-style auxiliary loss E·Σ fi·Pi (f = hard top-1 share, P = mean router probability; gradient flows through P).
