### 1. WHAT IS IT?

**Distillation** trains a **student** model to match a **teacher**, either on the teacher's outputs or on its soft token distributions.

### 2. WHY DOES IT EXIST?

To shrink a model for latency/cost, transfer a capability, or compress an ensemble or a post-trained model into a cheaper one.

### 3. DETAILED EXPLANATION

Two forms: *sequence-level* (SFT on teacher-generated text, like [10.7](10.07-synthetic-training-data.md)) and *logit distillation*: `KL(teacher || student)` on temperature-softened distributions, scaled by `T^2` (Hinton et al.). Limits: student capacity, teacher errors, and terms of use when the teacher is a vendor API (`REVIEW_REQUIRED`).

### 4. HOW DOES IT WORK?

`loss = T^2 * KL(softmax(z_t/T) || softmax(z_s/T))`, optionally plus hard-label CE.

### 5. SIMPLE EXAMPLE

A master craftsperson teaches an apprentice by showing finished work and also explaining how close the alternatives were.

### 6. TECHNICAL EXAMPLE

`distill_kl` is tested to be zero iff student and teacher logits are equal. No distilled-model result is reported.

### 7. CODE

```python
# lab/alignment.py
def distill_kl(student_logits, teacher_logits, T=2.0):
    p = F.softmax(teacher_logits / T, -1)
    return F.kl_div(F.log_softmax(student_logits / T, -1), p, reduction="batchmean") * T * T
```
File: [`lab/alignment.py`](lab/alignment.py).

### 8. INPUT → PROCESS → OUTPUT

```
teacher logits / outputs --> (soften with T) --> KL to student --> update student
```

### 9. ENTERPRISE USE CASE

Serve a small distilled model for cost while keeping most of a big model's behaviour on a narrow task.

### 10. AI ENGINEERING CONNECTION

Standard way to make cheap serving models from large post-trained ones ([Tier 11](../tier-11-llm-inference-optimization/README.md)).

### 11. ADVANTAGES

Cheaper inference; transfers behaviour.

### 12. LIMITATIONS

Student capacity bound; inherits teacher errors; legal terms.

### 13. TRADE-OFFS

Cost versus fidelity.

### 14. COMMON MISTAKES

Forgetting T^2; no held-out eval of the student; ignoring teacher licence.

### 15. PERFORMANCE / COST CONSIDERATIONS

Needs teacher inference for the data; training is a normal fine-tune.

### 16. SECURITY CONSIDERATIONS

Terms of service for vendor outputs; memorised teacher data may leak.

### 17. INTERVIEW EXPLANATION

Distillation trains a small student to imitate a teacher, either on its generated text or on its softened token distributions with a KL loss scaled by T squared. I use it to cut serving cost, with the caveats of student capacity, inherited errors and teacher terms of use.

### 18. RAPID-FIRE ANSWER

Distill: student copies teacher's distribution.
