# Tier 4 — author open items

## All 41 concepts (4.1–4.41) and the build

- TESTED: every runnable example on the 41 pages is executed by `tests/test_md_snippets.py` (output blocks are generated by `tools/md_snippets.py --fill`); `code/pipeline.py` has 11 tests and `lab/` 5 (16 in total, separate from the page-snippet tests). Environment: Python 3.14, PyTorch 2.14.1, NumPy 2.5.4, CPU only (no CUDA GPU was available).
- Honest results kept in the text: warm-up (4.11) tames the early spike on a toy network but does not improve the final loss; dropout/weight-decay-style gains are small; in 4.7 momentum β = 0.99 is worse than 0.9 on the valley; in 4.19 mixed precision does **not** shrink the weights+optimizer part of memory (16 bytes/parameter either way); resuming without optimizer state (4.20) follows a different path rather than being "worse".
- Process-level demos (4.30, 4.31, 4.34) fork two CPU processes with the `gloo` backend (`mp.start_processes(..., start_method="fork")`): works on macOS/Linux, not Windows.
- Fixed while authoring: the snippet tool's code-fence regex could swallow an unpaired fence in section 6 (stricter pattern added); `--fill` now rewrites exact output while `--check` tolerates small numeric differences across platforms.
