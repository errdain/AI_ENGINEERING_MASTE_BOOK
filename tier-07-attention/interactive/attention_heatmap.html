Attention heatmap & softmax saturation

Edit the sentence, change d_k, remove the 1/sqrt(d_k) scaling and watch the softmax collapse to one-hot. Toggle the causal mask and pick a query row to follow (scores → softmax → weights).

Q and K are random Gaussian vectors derived from the token text and seed (no trained model), so the point is the mechanics: raw dot-product variance grows like d_k; without the 1/sqrt(d_k) factor the softmax saturates (max weight to 1, entropy to 0), which also kills gradients. Fully masked rows would be NaN in a naive implementation; here the first causal row simply attends to itself.
