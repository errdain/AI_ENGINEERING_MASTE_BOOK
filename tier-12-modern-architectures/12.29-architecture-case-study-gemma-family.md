### 1. WHAT IS IT?

A labelled **case study** of the Gemma family as an architecture: which of this tier's durable ideas it uses. **Case study (REVIEW_REQUIRED):** every number below comes from the lab's `PRESETS` and must be checked against the official config/model card before external use; no vendor API is needed.

### 2. WHY DOES IT EXIST?

Named families are examples; the durable lessons are the design choices (the rule of this tier: families are case studies, ideas are authoritative).

### 3. DETAILED EXPLANATION

The Gemma family is a case study of open decoder family with alternating local/global layers, tied large-vocabulary embeddings.

**Features used (REVIEW_REQUIRED):** alternating sliding-window and global attention ([12.10 Sliding-window attention](12.10-sliding-window-attention.md)), GQA, head dim 256, tied embeddings with a very large vocabulary, gated FFN; other versions differ.
