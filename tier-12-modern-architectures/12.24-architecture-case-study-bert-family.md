### 2. WHY DOES IT EXIST?

Named families are examples; the durable lessons are the design choices (the rule of this tier: families are case studies, ideas are authoritative).

### 3. DETAILED EXPLANATION

The BERT family is a case study of encoder-only design, masked-language modelling, bidirectional attention.

**Features used:** encoder-only layout ([12.3 Encoder-only models as an architectural family](12.03-encoder-only-models-as-an-architectural-family.md)), learned absolute positions (Tier 6), non-gated 4× FFN, WordPiece tokenizer (Tier 6), `[CLS]`/pooled heads; later variants (e.g. RoBERTa-style) mainly change training recipe (REVIEW_REQUIRED).
