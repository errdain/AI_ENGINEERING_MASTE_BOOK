Fairness metric explorer: parity vs equalized odds

Two groups with different base rates. Set each group's TPR and FPR. Demographic parity compares selection rates; equalized odds compares TPR and FPR. Try TPR/FPR equal across groups: EO gap = 0 but DP gap is not (when base rates differ).

Selection rate = base·TPR + (1−base)·FPR. When base rates differ, equalizing selection rates generally requires unequal TPR or FPR, and vice versa (an impossibility-style tension; see Tier 15 concept 15.19). Thresholds for "acceptable gaps" are policy decisions, not constants.
