Calibration & reliability diagram

A synthetic 3-class model. Sharpen multiplies the logits (over/under-confidence) without changing accuracy. Temperature divides them back. ECE = Σ (n_b/N)·|acc_b − conf_b|.

Bars = accuracy in each confidence bin; diagonal = perfect calibration; bars below the diagonal = overconfident. Fewer examples ⇒ noisier bins (try n=100).
