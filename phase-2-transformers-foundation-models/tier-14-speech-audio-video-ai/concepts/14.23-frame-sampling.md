### 1. WHAT IS IT?

Frame sampling chooses which frames of a video to feed to a model: uniform-K (K evenly spaced frames), fixed stride (every Nth frame), target fps, random/segment-based (training), or content-adaptive (keyframes, shot-aware, motion-aware).

### 2. WHY DOES IT EXIST?

You cannot afford every frame: tokens, memory and money scale with frames. Sampling decides what information survives, so it directly sets accuracy, coverage of events, and cost.

### 3. DETAILED EXPLANATION

**Strategies (all in `video_core.py`).**
- `sample_uniform(n, k)`: indices at the centres of K equal segments. Fixed token budget, guarantees temporal coverage, but spacing grows with video length (long video = sparse sampling).
- `sample_stride(n, stride)`: every Nth frame. Constant temporal density; cost grows linearly with duration.
- `sample_fps(n, src_fps, target_fps)`: pick frames approximating e.g. 1 fps; the common choice for video-LLM ingestion (`REVIEW_REQUIRED` for current model defaults).
- *Segment-based random sampling* (TSN-style): one random frame per segment during training for augmentation.
- *Adaptive*: sample more around shot boundaries ([14.24](14.24-scene-shot-segmentation.md)) or high-motion regions; deduplicate near-identical frames.

**The sampling theorem returns.** Temporal sampling has a Nyquist-like limit: an event shorter than the gap between samples can be missed entirely (a 0.5 s gesture with 1 fps sampling), and fast periodic motion can alias (wheel illusion, [14.2](14.02-sampling-rate.md)). The explorer shows which shots a sampler misses.

**Budget arithmetic.** `tokens = frames x tokens_per_frame`. A 10-minute video at 1 fps with 256 tokens/frame is 153 600 tokens (illustrative arithmetic; exact budgets are model-specific, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`), which often exceeds a model's context, forcing lower fps, token pooling or hierarchical processing.

**Audio-aware sampling.** For videos where speech carries the content (lectures), sample by transcript segments ([14.29](14.29-audio-video-text-alignment.md)).

### 5. SIMPLE EXAMPLE

To summarise a film from a few screenshots, you can take one every ten minutes (uniform), or take more screenshots where the scene changes (adaptive).

### 6. TECHNICAL EXAMPLE

Uniform-8 on 90 frames gives [5, 16, 28, 39, 50, 61, 73, 84]; `sample_fps(300, 30, 1)` gives every 30th frame; the lab test `test_samplers` checks both.

### 7. CODE

```python
# run inside lab/
from video_core import sample_uniform, sample_stride, sample_fps, video_cost

print(sample_uniform(90, 8).tolist())          # [5, 16, 28, 39, 50, 61, 73, 84]
print(sample_stride(90, 15).tolist())          # [0, 15, 30, 45, 60, 75]
print(sample_fps(300, 30, 1).tolist()[:4])     # [0, 30, 60, 90]
for fps in (0.5, 1, 2):
    print(fps, video_cost(minutes=10, fps_sampled=fps, tokens_per_frame=256, usd_per_mtok=1.0))
```

### 9. ENTERPRISE USE CASE

Video-QA and moderation services choose fps per use case: 1 fps for general understanding, higher for sports or safety events; cost per minute follows directly from the sampling choice.

### 10. AI ENGINEERING CONNECTION

Frame sampling determines the visual token budget of video-language models ([14.28](14.28-video-language-models.md)) and is the main lever in video cost ([14.33](14.33-audio-video-performance-and-cost-considerations.md)).

### 11. ADVANTAGES

Large cost reduction with small accuracy loss when events are slow; easy to implement; tunable.

### 12. LIMITATIONS

Misses short events, aliases fast motion, uniform sampling thins out on long videos, adaptive methods need extra computation.

### 13. TRADE-OFFS

Coverage vs temporal resolution vs cost; fixed K (bounded cost) vs fixed fps (consistent density).

### 14. COMMON MISTAKES

- Uniform-K on hour-long videos and expecting fine detail.
- Sampling the first frame of a clip repeatedly (use segment centres).
- Different sampling in training and inference.
- Ignoring audio when the content is in speech.

### 15. PERFORMANCE / COST CONSIDERATIONS

Cost is linear in frames sampled; decoding sparse frames still pays seek costs; precompute and cache features for repeated queries.

### 16. SECURITY CONSIDERATIONS

Adversaries can hide content between sampled frames (a single-frame insertion) to evade moderation: randomise sampling offsets and sample densely around changes (`REVIEW_REQUIRED`).

### 17. INTERVIEW EXPLANATION

*"Frame sampling picks which frames reach the model. Uniform-K gives a fixed budget and coverage, fixed stride or fps keeps constant density, and adaptive methods sample around changes. The risk is missing short events and aliasing, so I pick based on event duration and budget, and I keep training and serving sampling identical."*

### 18. RAPID-FIRE ANSWER

Choose frames by uniform-K, stride, target fps or adaptively, trading coverage, temporal resolution and cost.
