### 3. DETAILED EXPLANATION

The Qwen family is a case study of open decoder family with aggressive GQA, large vocabulary and long context.

**Features used (REVIEW_REQUIRED):** GQA with few KV heads (4 in the 7B shape used here), SwiGLU, RoPE, long-context variants, MoE members in some releases, multilingual vocabulary.
