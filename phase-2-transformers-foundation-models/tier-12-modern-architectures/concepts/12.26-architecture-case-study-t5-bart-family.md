### 2. WHY DOES IT EXIST?

### 3. DETAILED EXPLANATION

The T5/BART family is a case study of encoder-decoder design with denoising objectives.

**Features used:** encoder-decoder layout ([12.5 Encoder-decoder models as an architectural family](12.05-encoder-decoder-models-as-an-architectural-family.md)), cross-attention, span-corruption/denoising pre-training (T5/BART, Tier 9), relative position bias in T5 (Tier 6), text-to-text framing (REVIEW_REQUIRED details).
