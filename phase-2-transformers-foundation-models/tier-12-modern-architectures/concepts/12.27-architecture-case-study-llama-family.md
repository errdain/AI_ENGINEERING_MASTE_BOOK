### 2. WHY DOES IT EXIST?

Named families are examples; the durable lessons are the design choices (the rule of this tier: families are case studies, ideas are authoritative).

### 3. DETAILED EXPLANATION

The Llama family is a case study of open decoder-only design that popularised RMSNorm + SwiGLU + RoPE, later GQA.

**Features used (REVIEW_REQUIRED per version):** pre-norm RMSNorm, SwiGLU FFN (3 matrices), RoPE ([12.8 RoPE variants and extensions](12.08-rope-variants-and-extensions.md)), GQA in later/larger members ([12.7 Grouped-Query Attention (GQA) in modern models](12.07-grouped-query-attention-gqa-in-modern-models.md)), large vocabulary growth between generations, long-context extensions via RoPE scaling.

### 6. TECHNICAL EXAMPLE

Llama-2-7B shape: MHA, 32 KV heads → 512 KiB/token, ≈6.74 B params. Llama-3-8B shape: 8 KV heads, d_ff 14336, vocab 128256 → 128 KiB/token, ≈8.03 B (lab; REVIEW_REQUIRED).
