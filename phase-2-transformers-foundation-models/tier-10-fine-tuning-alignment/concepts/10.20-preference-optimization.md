### 1. WHAT IS IT?

**Preference optimization** is the family of methods that move a policy toward chosen and away from rejected answers.

### 2. WHY DOES IT EXIST?

To optimise for "better", which SFT cannot express.

### 3. DETAILED EXPLANATION

Three families: **reward model + RL** (RLHF/PPO, [10.22](10.22-rlhf.md)); **direct** methods (DPO and relatives, [10.25](10.25-direct-preference-optimization-dpo.md)) that fold the reward into the policy; and **rejection-sampling fine-tuning** (sample many, keep the best by reward, SFT on those). Choose by data format (pairs vs scalar scores), infrastructure appetite, and need for online exploration.

![Pipeline](../figures/01_post_training_pipeline.png)

*Figure: both RLHF and DPO branches leave the SFT model and consume the same preference pairs.*

### 4. HOW DOES IT WORK?

Pairs -> (reward model) -> policy update under a closeness constraint to the reference model.

### 5. SIMPLE EXAMPLE

Steering an employee with feedback instead of a script.

### 6. TECHNICAL EXAMPLE

Lab: DPO on SFT-with-noise raises correct answers from 42 % to 70 % (76 % with an NLL anchor); see [10.25](10.25-direct-preference-optimization-dpo.md).

### 9. ENTERPRISE USE CASE

Application teams mostly use DPO-style methods; labs use RL with RMs and verifiers.

### 10. AI ENGINEERING CONNECTION

Shapes tone, safety and helpfulness of every assistant.

### 11. ADVANTAGES

Optimises relative quality directly.

### 12. LIMITATIONS

Depends on label quality; reward hacking or likelihood displacement.

### 13. TRADE-OFFS

Offline simplicity versus online exploration.

### 14. COMMON MISTAKES

Skipping SFT; ignoring length bias; optimising too far.

### 15. PERFORMANCE / COST CONSIDERATIONS

Ranges from ~2x SFT (DPO) to multiple model copies (PPO).

### 16. SECURITY CONSIDERATIONS

Preference tuning can shift safety behaviour either way; evaluate.

### 17. INTERVIEW EXPLANATION

Preference optimization teaches 'better versus worse'. The options are a reward model with RL, direct methods like DPO that skip the RM, or best-of-n SFT. I choose by data format, infrastructure and whether I need online exploration.

### 18. RAPID-FIRE ANSWER

Optimise 'better', not just 'correct text'.
