Catastrophic forgetting and weight merging (2-D toy)

A toy with two weights. The pretrained model sits at the optimum of old-skill loss L_A (blue ellipses). Fine-tuning runs gradient descent on the new-task loss L_B (orange) and drifts away from A's optimum. Replay adds lambda * L_A to the objective (step size is divided by 1+lambda to stay stable). Then interpolate weights between the base and the fine-tuned point. Illustrative quadratics, not a real network.

Things to try: (1) high learning rate or many steps: L_B ~ 0 but L_A grows = forgetting. (2) lambda > 0: you stop between the optima (compromise). (3) With lambda = 0, slide merge t: the curve shows the trade-off; an intermediate t often keeps most of the new skill at much lower old-skill loss - in the toy because the curves are convex; real networks need not behave this way (see the lab figure for a measured counter-example).
