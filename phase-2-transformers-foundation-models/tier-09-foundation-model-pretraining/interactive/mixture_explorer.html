Language balancing (9.7): temperature sampling

Sample language i with probability proportional to n_i^α. α=1 follows the raw data (large languages dominate); α→0 is uniform (small languages are repeated many times).

Source names and sizes are made up to show the mechanics. The "4 epochs is still useful" threshold is a rule of thumb from published repeated-data studies (REVIEW_REQUIRED, depends on model and data). Weights are fractions of training tokens, not documents.
