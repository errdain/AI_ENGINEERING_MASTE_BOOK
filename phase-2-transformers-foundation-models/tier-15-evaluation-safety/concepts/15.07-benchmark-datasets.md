### 1. WHAT IS IT?

Benchmark datasets are the public or private test collections used to compare models (knowledge QA, reasoning, code, truthfulness, safety, bias).

### 2. WHY DOES IT EXIST?

Shared datasets give comparability across labs and over time; domain datasets give relevance. Knowing what each family measures and where it fails prevents misuse.

### 3. DETAILED EXPLANATION

Families and well-known examples (names only; details and current status REVIEW_REQUIRED):

| Family | Examples | Measures | Typical caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knowledge / multi-task | MMLU | broad factual and academic knowledge | multiple choice; saturation; label errors |
| Commonsense | HellaSwag | plausible continuation | saturated |
| Math reasoning | GSM8K, MATH | grade-school/competition math | contamination; answer-format sensitivity |
| Code | HumanEval, MBPP | unit-test pass rate (pass@k) | small; leakage |
| Truthfulness | TruthfulQA | imitation of common misconceptions | narrow; adversarial by design |
| Bias | BBQ and similar | stereotype reliance in QA | templated |
| Safety | red-team and harm prompt sets | refusal/harm | coverage; subjective labels |

Private, domain-specific sets (your tickets, your documents) usually predict your product better than any public set. Tag each item with source, date and licence.

### 4. HOW DOES IT WORK?

Choose datasets per objective ([15.2 Evaluation objectives](15.02-evaluation-objectives.md)), run under a fixed protocol, report with intervals, and cross-check with a private set.

### 5. SIMPLE EXAMPLE

Public exams let you compare students across schools; an interview with your own problems tells you who suits your company.

### 6. TECHNICAL EXAMPLE

A model scores high on a public math set but poorly on your finance word problems: the public set measures a different distribution ([15.17 Distribution-shift evaluation](15.17-distribution-shift-evaluation.md)).

### 10. AI ENGINEERING CONNECTION

Dataset choice defines what 'better model' means for foundation-model selection; Tier 30 covers application datasets.

### 11. ADVANTAGES

- Comparability and history.
- Cheap to run.

### 12. LIMITATIONS

- Saturation, contamination, narrow construct, licence limits.

### 13. TRADE-OFFS

- Public comparability vs private relevance.

### 14. COMMON MISTAKES

- Choosing by leaderboard rank alone.
- Ignoring licences and provenance.

### 15. PERFORMANCE / COST CONSIDERATIONS

Large suites (thousands of items x many models) can be expensive; sample for iteration.

### 16. SECURITY CONSIDERATIONS

Public items are likely in training corpora; use private sets for decisions ([15.21 Memorization/data-leakage evaluation](15.21-memorization-data-leakage-evaluation.md)).

### 17. INTERVIEW EXPLANATION

Benchmark datasets cover knowledge, reasoning, code, truthfulness, bias and safety. They allow comparison but saturate and leak, so I combine public sets with a private domain set and always check contamination.

### 18. RAPID-FIRE ANSWER

Public for comparison, private for decisions.
