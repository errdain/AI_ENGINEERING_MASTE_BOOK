Weight quantization: rounding error you can see

Symmetric quantization: q = round(w / s), s = max|w| / (2^(b−1)−1) per group; w' = q·s. Rounding error is at most s/2 inside the range. One outlier inflates s for its whole group and hurts everyone else, which is why group-wise scales help.

Histogram of the quantization error (w' − w). A uniform error in ±s/2 is the signature of rounding.
